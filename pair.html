Step 1 · Your sync account

Create or sign in

This is the account your devices share. Use any email you control and a strong password — the password becomes half of your pairing code, and it's what stands between the public internet and your data.

Step 2 · Your pairing code

Paste this on every device

Open any tracker on a device, click the sync chip in the corner, and paste this in. Do it once per device — phone, laptop, tablet.

This code contains your password. Treat it like one: don't post it anywhere public, and don't paste it on a device you don't control.
